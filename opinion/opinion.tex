\section*{1\quad Background}
\para A review of item Q015 was requested after examinees reported two
defensible answers. This opinion assesses the item using content review and
post-administration statistics.

\section*{2\quad Analysis}
\para The item showed a corrected point-biserial of $r = -0.23$, meaning that
higher-scoring examinees were \emph{less} likely to choose the published key.

\para Option analysis shows that 61\% of the top third chose option~A, while the
published key is option~B.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{c r r r}
\toprule
\textbf{Option} & \textbf{Low group} & \textbf{Middle} & \textbf{High group} \\
\midrule
A & 18\% & 39\% & \textbf{61\%} \\
B (published key) & 46\% & 41\% & 29\% \\
C & 21\% & 12\% & 6\% \\
D & 15\% & 8\% & 4\% \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\para Independent content review by two experts confirmed that option~A is the
correct answer according to current guidelines.

\section*{3\quad Opinion}
\para The item is \textbf{miskeyed}. It is recommended that:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*),nosep]
  \item the key be changed to option A and the exam re-scored;
  \item the item be returned to the author for revision of the distractors;
  \item the review checklist be applied to all items from the same author.
\end{enumerate}
